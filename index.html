About

I am a PhD student in computer science at the University of Science and Technology of China, in Hefei, since September 2026. My work is computer vision, medical image analysis, deep learning, and vision-language models: object detection, image segmentation, anomaly detection, and systems that have to run in real time.

Before the doctorate I was a master’s student in the AIoT Lab at USTC, and earlier a research assistant at Southwest University of Science and Technology. Several of the papers are in peer-reviewed venues. Two more are under review.

Away from the lab I volunteer, play cricket and football, read case studies, and spend time with people who are serious about their own fields. That is usually where the next idea starts.
