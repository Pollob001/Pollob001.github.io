About me

I am a PhD student in computer science at the University of Science and Technology of China, in Hefei, since September 2026. I work on medical images that do not sit still: blurred boundaries, dim scans, and models that still have to be right when the data changes.

My work is computer vision, medical image analysis, deep learning, and vision-language models: object detection, image segmentation, anomaly detection, and systems that have to run in real time. Before the doctorate I was a master’s student in the AIoT Lab at USTC, and earlier a research assistant at Southwest University of Science and Technology. Several of the papers are in peer-reviewed venues. You can read the CV in English or 中文.

Selected Papers

  • HyperSeg-DG: Multi-Scale Hyper-Feature Context for Domain-Generalized Medical Image Segmentation. Bioinformatics · CCF-A
    Md Aynul Islam. A hypergraph-enhanced visual perception framework for segmentation under domain shift, low light, low contrast, and blurred anatomical boundaries. Supported by NSFC 62502491 and the USTC Software Youth Fund YN2260080011.
  • Lung Nodule Classification with Quantum Neural Networks and Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks. IEEE RAAICON 2025
    Md Aynul Islam. Studies how quantum neural networks and Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks can be combined to classify lung nodules.
  • Integrating YOLOv8 and Vision Transformers: An Enhanced Security Framework for Digital Twins. IEEE RAAICON 2025
    Md Aynul Islam. A real-time security framework using YOLOv8, vision transformers, DeepSORT, IoT data, and a digital twin for activity and threat detection. 93.77% accuracy and 98.14% precision on the Real-Life Violence dataset.
  • An Edge-Cloud Collaborative Autonomous Driving System Based on Swin-YOLOv11. IEEE PRECEDE
    Md Aynul Islam. An edge-cloud detection framework built on Swin-YOLOv11 to raise accuracy without giving up computational efficiency.
