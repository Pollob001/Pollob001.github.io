01 / PROFILE

Researching where vision models become difficult.

My work focuses on systems that still need to perform when images are dim, boundaries are unclear, domains shift, or the data does not look like the training set.

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China, in Hefei, since September 2026.

My research spans computer vision, medical image analysis, deep learning, and vision-language models, including object detection, image segmentation, anomaly detection, and real-time intelligent systems.

Before the doctorate I was a master’s student in the AIoT Lab at USTC and earlier a research assistant at Southwest University of Science and Technology. Away from the lab, I volunteer, play cricket and football, and enjoy learning from people working deeply in other fields.
